# Customer marks

The owner supplied these companies and projects as users of tend. The shared
list and localized introduction live in `site/build.py`; no results or
testimonials are inferred from the customer websites.

Assets were obtained from the brands' own websites on 2026-10-14:

| Mark | Source | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RVX Telecom | https://rvxtelecom.com/images/logo.png | Original PNG; grayscale applied in CSS. |
| União Maker | https://uniaomaker.com.br/images/logo-horizontal.svg | Original geometry and lettering; cropped SVG viewBox, white ink, badge details cut out with a mask. |
| Maestre | https://maestre.com.br/images/png/maestre-logo-menu.png | Original PNG; grayscale, contrast and screen blending applied in CSS. |
| JSouza Net | https://jsouzanet.com.br/medias/JSOUZANET.svg | Original paths with white fills and a cropped viewBox. |
| Retro Studio | https://retrostudio.dev/ | The header's RETRO STUDIO wordmark, outlined from the site's Orbitron Bold with its letter spacing. |
| TV Connect USA | https://tvconnectusa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/logotipo-tv-connect-usa.webp | Original WebP; white monochrome applied in CSS. |

Retro Studio's font source was
https://retrostudio.dev/_nuxt/Orbitron-normal-400-latin.Du7j2Vp2.woff2.
Its SIL Open Font License is included in `Orbitron-OFL.txt`. All marks belong
